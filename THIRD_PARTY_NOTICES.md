[hanzi-writer-data 2.0.1](https://github.com/chanind/hanzi-writer-data), which
comes from [Make Me a Hanzi](https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi).
The graphics are derived from fonts by Arphic Technology Co., Ltd.
Copyright (C) 1999 Arphic Technology Co., Ltd.

This data is distributed under the Arphic Public License, reproduced in full
in `src/hanzi/ARPHICPL.TXT` and copied into both distribution directories as
`ARPHICPL.TXT`. The subset preserves the original stroke outlines and medians;
the game transforms their coordinates when drawing and matching strokes.
See `src/hanzi/README.md` for the fixed source archive and reproduction steps.
